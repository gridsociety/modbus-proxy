## Limits

No field installation, all-13-port hardware qualification, prolonged soak,
real-device timing qualification, or monitoring/watchdog installation was done.
These short deterministic tests are not a certification of indefinite operation.
The POSIX descriptor test is skipped on Windows; no tests were skipped in the
reported macOS runs. Linux CI is configured separately; local results above do
not claim a completed CI run. Protocol-correlation and ambiguous-write retry
semantics remain separate issues documented in `docs/resource-lifecycle.md`.
